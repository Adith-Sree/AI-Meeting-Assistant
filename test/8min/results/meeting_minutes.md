## Meeting Summary
The Oregon Tourism Commission Executive Director Search Subcommittee met on April 29 to approve prior meeting minutes and to advance five candidates to the second round of interviews for the Executive Director position. The subcommittee also noted a recusal by Vice Chair Patel and confirmed that upcoming executive sessions and public meetings will be properly noticed.

## Meeting Minutes
1. **Call to Order & Introductions** – Chair Greg Willet opened the meeting, introduced attendees (Commissioners DeNovo, Grimble, Hill, Vice Chair Patel, DOJ representative Sylvia Kramond, and Heidrick & Struggles team members Jackie Zavitz, Molly Moore, Beth Easterly).  
2. **Public Meeting Notice Confirmation** – Confirmation that the April 27 notice satisfied state law requirements.  
3. **Review of Prior Minutes** – Commissioner Patel reported that drafts of the April 2, 16, and 17 minutes were emailed on April 27. No comments were received.  
4. **Approval of Prior Minutes** – Motion by Commissioner Grimble, seconded by Commissioner Patel; roll‑call vote passed unanimously.  
5. **Executive Session Disclosure** – Statement that an executive session was held earlier per ORS 192.6602(a); no decisions were made.  
6. **Recusal Statement** – Commissioner Patel recused herself from decisions related to the first‑round interviews.  
7. **Advancement of Candidates** – Motion to advance David Hall, Kate Sinner, Carmen Foer, Kevin Dugan, and Stacy Shaw to the second interview round; seconded by Commissioner DeNovo; roll‑call vote passed unanimously.  
8. **Future Meetings** – Announcement that the subcommittee will hold additional executive sessions and public meetings in the coming weeks, with proper notice.  
9. **Adjournment** – Meeting adjourned by Chairman Patel.

## Key Decisions
- Approved the minutes from the April 2, 16, and 17 Executive Director Search Subcommittee meetings.  
- Approved advancing David Hall, Kate Sinner, Carmen Foer, Kevin Dugan, and Stacy Shaw to the second round of interviews.  
- Noted Vice Chair Patel’s recusal from first‑round interview decisions.  
- Confirmed that no action was taken during the earlier executive session.
